Research Projects

Sustainable Wearable Electronics — Thermoelectric Power Systems

University of Maryland, Baltimore County — July 2025 – August 2026 · Baltimore, MD

Designed a PCB/IC platform on a breadboard that generates an electrical pulse every few minutes using thermoelectric generators (TEGs)—sufficient to power a wearable electrocardiograph long enough to capture a reading. Led PCB fabrication and tested 10 trials across 4 TEG configurations with 3+ controlled environmental variables to identify the most efficient array.

Published as: "Utilization of Commercial Peltier Thermoelectric Generators for Continuous Wearable Device Use," Oxford Journal of Student Scholarship, June 2026. Found no significant gain from series TEGs but a clear improvement from heat sinks. Read the publication →

Calcium Manganese Oxide Thin Films

Towson University — July 2025 – August 2025 · Towson, MD

Fabricated 10 CMO thin-film metal-oxide samples via pulsed laser deposition and measured electrical resistance with the four-point probe method. Gained ISO 7 clean-room experience while fluorinating 5+ films by spin-coating. Collaborated with graduate researchers to correlate structural properties (X-ray diffraction) with electrical transport behavior.

Exoplanet Light Curve Validation (TOI 5868.01)

George Mason University — Summer 2024 · Fairfax, VA

Examined ground-based light-curve data from the George Mason Telescope with AstroImageJ to validate a TESS Object of Interest as a candidate exoplanet. Built a Python Jupyter Notebook pipeline that formatted 100+ images into light-curve data and performed the preliminary analysis of TOI 5868.01.

Published as: "Ground-Based Light Curve Follow-Up Validation Observations of TESS Object of Interest (TOI) 5868.01," George Mason MARS Repository, August 2024. Concluded a high likelihood that TOI 5868.01 is a genuine exoplanet, while noting further work is needed to rule out a false positive. Read the publication →
